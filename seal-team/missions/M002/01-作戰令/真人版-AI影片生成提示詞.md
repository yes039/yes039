共同設定（每條提示詞都要附上）：
`vertical 9:16, photorealistic, shot on 35mm, natural skin texture, soft film grain, shallow depth of field, Taiwan, no text, no logos`

`Taiwanese woman about 66 years old, short grey permed hair, thin gold-rimmed glasses, warm kind face with natural wrinkles, mauve knitted cardigan over cream blouse, small pearl earrings`
